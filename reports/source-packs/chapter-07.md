## No-equivalent notes

- Tip 07-1: no direct Indian equivalent of contributory unemployment insurance with
  statutory 12/18/24-month benefit durations. Closest Indian route: ESIC's Atal Beemit
  Vyakti Kalyan Yojana, EPF withdrawal, EPS withdrawal benefit, MGNREGA, state welfare boards.
- Tip 07-4: no statutory 10-day shelter admission or free travel voucher. Closest Indian
  route: DAY-NULM rain baseras, state destitute homes, One Stop Centres, 181, 1098, 112.
- Tip 07-6: no national emergency cash assistance claimable by any resident. Closest Indian
  route: SDRF relief through the district collector (notified disasters only), Chief
  Minister's Relief Fund, NFBS death benefit, PM-JAY for the medical bill.
- Tip 07-7: no cash payment equal to a poverty-line gap; NFSA entitlement is grain in kind.
- Tip 07-8: no paired living-plus-care subsidy; India uses UDID plus IGNDPS/state pension
  plus state caregiver allowances where they exist.
- Tip 07-12: no employment-difficulty recognition carrying a social-insurance subsidy.
  Closest routes: e-Shram, NCS, welfare board membership, BPL/AAY card, UDID.
- Tip 07-15: no general statutory ban on recruitment fees and no four-times-LPR interest cap.
- Tip 07-16: no statutory waiting-period guarantee for public rental housing.
- Tip 07-18: no fifteen-year pension threshold; the EPS threshold is ten years.
- Tip 07-19: no operational personal bankruptcy; IBC Part III is dormant.
- Tip 07-21: no legal 24-hour refuge and no official safety data for station floors.
- Tip 07-22: no national domestic workers statute; most labour Acts exclude private households.
- Tip 07-23: no statutory right to a five-year instalment plan.

## Open issues

- labour.gov.in, pib.gov.in, india.gov.in and nsap.nic.in refused or failed this client on
  21 October 2026 (403, 403, 403, DNS). Rows marked **supported (blocked)** rest on the
  statute or scheme text, not on a page fetched that day. Re-fetch before review.
- The four labour codes' commencement (21 November 2025) is taken from the notification
  linked in data/india-context.json. The Codes' own section numbers used here (Code on
  Wages claims authority; OSH Code absorption of the ISMW Act) still need a line-by-line
  check against the gazette text.
- The Code on Wages limitation period is deliberately not stated as a number; the tip says
  it differs from the old Acts and must be confirmed. Resolve before review.
- ABVKY's current rate, duration and whether claims are open are not stated; only its
  existence and contributory conditions are. ESIC circular needed.
- NSAP central rates (IGNOAPS, IGNWPS, IGNDPS, NFBS) are deliberately not quoted because
  nsap.nic.in did not resolve. Add them only from the NSAP page or a PIB release.
- PM-JAY's 70+ universal cover is stated from the NHA FAQ title and 2024 launch; confirm the
  current eligibility wording and the Rs 5 lakh ceiling on nha.gov.in.
- HCES 2022-23 shares (46% rural, 39% urban food) are rounded from the survey fact sheet and
  were not re-fetched; confirm against mospi.gov.in before review.
- NAPS 25%/Rs 1,500 figures and the 36-month pre-existing waiting-period cap need the
  current NAPS guideline and IRDAI regulation number respectively.
- Tip 07-14 keeps a non-Indian evidence base (47 studies, mostly Europe and North America).
  The caveat says so. No Indian replication was found.
- No hotline, rupee amount or section number in the chapter was invented. Where a number
  could not be confirmed, the text points to the authority instead of quoting it.
