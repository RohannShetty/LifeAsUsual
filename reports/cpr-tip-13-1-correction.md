# Tip 13-1 CPR correction log

Date: 31 October 2026  
Status: **Editorial correction; no clinical specialist sign-off.**

## Why the old copy was changed

The inherited India tip said "79.2% of cardiac arrests happen at home" without indicating that the figure was taken from a non-Indian population. The sentence went further and inferred that most people a reader would assist must be family members. Neither conclusion was established for India by the cited source.

The old copy also told readers to start compressions **before** having someone call 112 and said they "must not stop compressing", even when an AED may instruct a short pause. This was too absolute for a safety-critical guide.

## Corrections made

- Removed the 79.2% statistic and the family-member inference from the reader-facing summary.
- Added an adult/teen scope and the distinction between no normal breathing and gasping.
- Placed emergency calling (India: 112) and AED instructions in the immediate response.
- Used the American Heart Association's 100–120 compressions/minute advice and instruction to follow the AED's prompts.
- Replaced regulator homepages that supported no specific CPR claim with a direct source for the emergency response and a qualified CPR source.
- Preserved the older survival review as historical research, explicitly **not** an India-wide outcome estimate.
- Removed unsourced CPR-course cost and long-lived-certification promises.

## Sources used for this revision

1. American Heart Association, [Treatment of Cardiac Arrest](https://www.heart.org/en/health-topics/cardiac-arrest/emergency-treatment-of-cardiac-arrest), page reviewed **14 September 2026**.

3. Sasson C et al. (2010), [Predictors of survival from out-of-hospital cardiac arrest](https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCOUTCOMES.109.889576), cited by the inherited lesson.
