## Finding

The previous India-edition text said EPF could pay rent, repay a housing loan, cover medical bills and several life events. It cited generic EPFO/Labour/Income-tax homepages and named the Employees' Provident Funds Scheme, 1952. Those sources did not identify the current purpose-specific rules. The inherited source title refers to a housing provident-fund system, not an Indian EPF statute.

The readable Government of India backgrounder dated 31 October 2026 identifies the current framework as the Employees' Provident Funds Scheme, 2026 under the Code on Social Security, 2020. It describes broad partial-withdrawal categories including **housing, higher education, marriage and medical treatment**, subject to prescribed conditions. It also reports a **12-month minimum service** and a limit of **up to 75%** of the PF balance. It does not establish that rent, a home-loan EMI or every housing expense qualifies, and it does not provide the complete purpose-specific tests.

The PIB backgrounder contains an apparent drafting error in its description of the superseded scheme name. This report does not repeat that statement or rely on it to determine repeal/commencement. The full statutory EPF Scheme 2026 text was not readable from the official source path during this pass; the EPFO portal returned an access error in the retrieval environment.

A separate PIB release dated 19 November 2026 reports that the Central Board approved reducing the minimum retained balance from **25% to 10%** and reducing the final-settlement waiting period from **12 months to 2 months**. The release does not provide an effective date or the implementing amendment. The existing cross-tip audit records that no notified amendment was visible on the EPFO circulars page as of 19 November. Therefore this tip does not present the 10% threshold as an operational entitlement. The final-settlement waiting-period change belongs to Chapter 7 tips 7-1/7-12 and must not be conflated with this partial-withdrawal tip.

## Changes proposed in the India edition

- Remove unsupported promises about rent and housing-loan repayment.
- Narrow the text to the broad categories stated in the 31 October Government summary.
- Keep purpose-specific eligibility, documentation and current limits conditional on the latest EPFO instructions and the member's own eligibility.
- Record the 19 November Board approval as a dated announcement whose effective date was not established, not as a guaranteed current rule.
- Retain the warning that partial withdrawals reduce retirement savings.
- Keep the legacy ID, route, anchor and reading-progress key unchanged.
