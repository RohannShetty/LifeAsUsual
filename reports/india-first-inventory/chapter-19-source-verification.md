## Left unverified

- “48-hour week” as a legal figure: it now appears only as “the familiar 48-hour week comes from the rules”, because the central rules could not be opened; state rules may differ.
- EPF's exact position after 21 November 2025: PIB release 2192463 does not address the EPF Act, and the commencement notification PDFs on egazette.gov.in render as an HTML embed with no extractable text. The chapter therefore asserts only the current administration and flags the transition.
- Section 27 of the OSH Code (overtime rate) could not be read in the fetched text, so the tips do not cite it.
- Income-tax treatment in 19-18 rests on the Income-tax Act, 1961; the Income-tax Act, 2025 is in force from 1 April 2026 and that section number needs re-checking before the tip is treated as current.
- Probability-of-injury schedules for ESIC (19-14) and ESIC death benefits (19-15) were not fetched.
- Negative claims in 19-3, 19-17, 19-20 and 19-23 (“no such rule”) are reasoned, not proved; they remain phrased as absence of a located rule.

## Internal consistency

19-7 and 19-21 previously implied different statutory homes for EPF; both now say the EPF Act and Scheme continue, with the Code governing ESIC and the transition noted. 19-13 still omits the 50-worker threshold that 19-24 states; that stays open as an editorial HOLD rather than a silent fix.
