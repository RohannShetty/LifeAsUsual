## Method

The eleven HIGH RISK rows in the pack (5-1, 5-3, 5-9, 5-10, 5-13, 5-31, 5-36, 5-39, 5-46, 5-47, 5-49) plus every other tip that cites a statute, regulator, threshold, hotline or fine was checked against the Indian instrument or authority. This chapter is the worst in the assigned range: fourteen tips still describe Chinese tax, telecom, procurement, insurance, vehicle, certification, capital-control, consumer and tourism law under rupee signs. Rupee conversion at a fixed 14.21 factor has been applied to Chinese yuan thresholds, which produces figures such as ₹17,05,200 and ₹71,05,000 that correspond to no Indian statute.
