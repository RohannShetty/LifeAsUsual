## Non-blocking notes

1. 28-6, benefit: the recording duty is rule 65(3)(1), but only the retention rule 65(7) is cited parenthetically; adding "(rule 65(3)(1))" after "recorded in a prescription register" would make the pair exact. The sources line for this tip also lists the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 without the Rules, 1945, although the whole claim lives in the Rules; 28-5's source line does cite the Rules and could be copied.
2. 28-5, caveats: "any product described with that phrase contains them" - a product advertised as an anabolic steroid may contain a different substance or none. The safe advice (treat it as a steroid) is unaffected, but "any product sold as one should be treated as one" would be accurate where "contains them" is not.
3. 28-5, benefit and inPlainWords: the paper reports "hospital contacts", not "hospital visits". "Visits" is a fair lay rendering, but "contacts" is the paper's term.
4. 28-10, inPlainWords: "a corticosteroid in a cosmetic is not permitted at all" is correct in effect but is not a provision of the Cosmetics Rules, 2020, whose rule 39(5) is the mercury provision quoted correctly elsewhere in the tip. The accurate basis is classification: a cream containing a corticosteroid is a drug under section 3(b) of the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and cannot lawfully be sold as a cosmetic.
5. 28-9, benefit and inPlainWords: the Medical Devices Rules, 2017 define a custom-made device by a "registered medical practitioner's" written prescription; "registered dentist's written prescription" is right in practice but not the defined term. The earlier overreach (that clear aligners are, as a class, a notified medical device) remains gone.
6. 28-9, benefit: "bars anyone who is not a registered dentist from practising dentistry" compresses section 32(1), which bars practising "as a qualified dentist". The substance is right; the qualifier could be carried if the sentence is revised.
7. 28-2, benefit: "give the district authority power to inspect and to close an unregistered one" - the 2010 Act's express power to restrain a clinical establishment from carrying on (s.32(3) proviso) attaches to cancellation of a registration; an unregistered establishment is met with entry and search (s.34) and a monetary penalty (s.41). The practical advice is unaffected, but "close" overstates the express remedy.
8. 28-1, caveats, and 28-3, caveats, still use the source edition's section vocabulary ("Section 2, Item 32", "Section 2 of this chapter") where the edition now numbers tips. 28-1's reference does resolve to chapter 2 tip 2-32, and 28-3's to tip 28-2.
