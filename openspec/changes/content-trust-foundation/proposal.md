# Proposal: Content Trust Foundation

## Why
The inherited India edition has 34 chapters and 765 tips, currently verified primarily at chapter level. A passing source-host check does not prove that a particular legal, health or financial claim has support. Public "Checked" labels do not clearly distinguish AI evaluation from professional review. A static number of original lessons cannot represent the uncapped India-first content approved in epic #4.

## What changes
- Introduce a canonical lesson and claim-evidence model without disrupting the existing 765 stable anchors.
- Separate original source provenance from India-specific adaptation, jurisdiction overlays, translations and publication status.
- Give every substantial claim an exact source locator, evidence/authority classification, scope, checked date and review status.
- Introduce distinct publication status labels: unverified; AI-reviewed draft; qualified human-reviewed; withdrawn.
- Use risk-sensitive publishing gates and independent verification with fail-closed behavior for unresolved safety-critical claims.
- Enable new India-original lesson IDs and truthful coverage counts without breaking the legacy schema or reading memory.

## Capabilities
### New: content-trust
Source-backed claims and review status, risk gates, editorial lifecycle, provenance.
### Modified: india-edition
Editorial labels, source verification and expandable content totals.

## Out of scope
Full 36-jurisdiction content, seven-language rollout, PWA/audio, rebrand and wholesale rewriting of 765 tips. Those follow the trust contract.

## Dependencies
Initial release stability PR #1 and security triage #2. No expert signoff is implied by this proposal.

## Done when
A representative original India lesson, a state-specific variation and a translated draft can pass or fail independent verification with an auditable evidence record, clear reader status and preserved legacy links. See issue #5.
