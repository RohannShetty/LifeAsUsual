## How reports become entries

1. A correction report is opened through the issue form at `/corrections/`.
2. The claim is checked against the official source named in the report.
3. If supported, the tip and its source link are fixed and a row is added here with the date checked.
4. If the source cannot be read or does not settle the claim, the tip stays unverified and the report is closed with that reason, not silently dropped.

Unverifiable and open review items are tracked separately in `reports/india-first-inventory/` and are not presented here as resolved.
